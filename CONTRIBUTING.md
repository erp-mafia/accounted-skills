# Contributing

Thank you for sharing what you know. There are two ways in, and both end in the same review.

## From Accounted

Open your own instruction under **Instruktioner → Egna** and choose **Dela**. Accounted opens it as a pull request here, reviews it, and once merged it appears on [accounted.se/instruktioner](https://accounted.se/instruktioner) and in every company's Upptäck.

## On GitHub

Add a folder under [`community/`](community/README.md) with a `SKILL.md` in the format described there, and open a pull request. Corrections to Accounted's own skills in `.claude/skills/` are welcome too.

## Review

Accounted reviews every pull request before it is merged: that nothing identifies a real company or person, that the content agrees with Swedish rules, and that an AI following it never books, sends or files anything without approval. We may suggest changes, or decline something that is too specific to one company.
